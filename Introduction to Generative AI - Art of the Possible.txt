###Overview of ML###

Introduction
Generative Al is a branch of machine learning (ML) that addresses the development of algorithms that can
create natural language text, images, code, audio, or videos based on user input. Generative Al has numerous
applications in various industries, such as media, entertainment, healthcare, and finance. In this lesson, you
learn about the relationship between ML and generative Al. You also get an introduction to generative Al and
how businesses derive business value from the use of generative Al.

Machine learning

To better understand generative Al, you need to understand ML. You might ask yourself, "How do machines
learn?" ML trains a computer to recognize patterns in historical data to make predictions on new data. These
predictions help you take business actions. ML works as follows:

![Flowchart of a Simple ML Task](https://skillbuilder.aws/cds/CONTENT_VBHN7H4FNT_002.002.001/assets/images/6716678c-9fe2-4a08-a2a2-38fb9ff539cf.jpeg)

You use a dataset to train a model. In this dataset, there are features and labels. The goal is to take the
features as inputs and find a formula that predicts the labels, or outputs. The resulting ML algorithms can take
new data, recognize patterns in the data, apply the formula, and make predictions about the data.

ML trains a computer to recognize patterns in historical data to mkae predictions on new data.

Understanding the Difference Between Generative Al and Traditional
ML
Generative Al is a type of artificial intelligence that can create new content, including text, images, code, and
audio. Generative Al is built on deep learning architectures and is powered by foundation models (FMs), large
models pretrained on internet-scale data. Unlike traditional deep learning applications that are trained for a
single specific task, foundation models can be adapted to perform multiple tasks, often through natural
language prompts alone and without the need for task-specific retraining.

Deep learning uses the concept of neurons and synapses similar to how your brain works. An example of a
deep learning application is Amazon Rekognition, which can analyze millions of images, streaming and stored
videos within seconds. Amazon Q Developer, an example of a generative Al application, can generate code
suggestions in real time based on your comments and existing code.

FMs can work across a variety of modalities,
including text, images, code, and audio. Large
language models (LLMs) are one type of foundation
model. With FMs, instead of gathering labeled data
for each model and training multiple models as in
traditional ML, customers can adapt the same FM to
perform multiple tasks.

LLMs have the ability to predict the next word in a
sentence by taking into consideration the position
and the context of a word in a sentence. LLMs use
this ability to generate new content.

###Geneartive AI Use Cases###

Introduction
Generative Al has the potential to bring about sweeping changes to the global economy. Goldman Sachs
estimates that about two-thirds of US occupations will be enhanced by Al. Al will likely drive the creation of
new jobs and the emergence of new occupations for the vast majority of long-run employment growth.

The future forecasts to be a period of intense experimentation. It is expected that new architectures will arise

Use Cases for Businesses
The following information explores six common industry-based uses cases.

##Healthcare##

Generative Al contributes to healthcare in the following ways:

. AWS HealthScribe: Empowers healthcare software vendors to build clinical applications
that automatically generate clinical notes by analyzing patient-clinician conversations.
. Personalized medicine: By generating treatment plans based on a patient's specific genetic
makeup, lifestyle, and disease progression, Al can contribute to more effective,
personalized care.
. Medical imaging: Al can enhance, reconstruct, or even generate medical images, like X-rays,
MRIs, or CT scans, which can aid in better diagnosis.

##Life Sciences##
Generative Al contributes to life sciences in the following ways:

· Drug discovery: Al can generate new potential molecular structures for drugs, accelerating
the process of drug discovery and reducing costs.
. Protein folding prediction: Al can predict the 3D structures of proteins based on their
amino acid sequence, which is crucial for understanding diseases and developing new
therapies.
. Synthetic biology: Al can generate designs for synthetic biological systems, such as
engineered organisms or biological circuits.

##Financial Services##
Generative Al contributes to financial services in the following ways:

. Fraud detection mechanisms: Generative Al can help create synthetic datasets to improve
Al/ML systems by simulating various money-laundering patterns.
. Portfolio management: Generative Al can simulate various market scenarios and help in
the creation and management of robust investment portfolios.
. Debt collection: Al can generate the most effective communication and negotiation
strategies for debt collection, increasing the rate of successful collections.

Across the banking industry, for example, Al technology can deliver value equal to an additional
$200-$340 billion annually if the use cases above are fully implemented (McKinsey Report 2023).

##Manufacturing##
Generative Al contributes to manufacturing in the following ways:

. Product design: Generative Al can be used to create new product designs based on set
parameters and constraints. It can generate multiple design options and optimize for
factors like cost, materials, performance, and so forth.
. Process optimization: Al can generate the most efficient production processes by modeling
different scenarios and optimizing for variables such as cost, time, resource usage, and so
forth.
. Preventative maintenance: By analyzing historical production data, Al can predict
maintenance schedules that will provide the most efficient machine outputs and reduce
downtimes.
. Material science: Al can help generate new material compositions with desired properties.

##Retail##
Generative Al contributes to retail in the following ways:

. Pricing optimization: Generative Al can model different pricing scenarios to determine
optimal pricing strategies that maximize profits.
. Virtual try-ons: Al can generate virtual models of customers for virtual try-ons, improving
the online shopping experience.
. Store layout optimization: Al can generate the most efficient store layouts to improve the
customer shopping experience and boost sales.
. Product review summaries: Al can generate review summaries for products so consumers
can quickly find pertinent information.

In retail and consumer packaged goods, the potential generative Al impact is significant at $400
billion to $660 billion a year. (McKinsey Report 2023).

##Media and Entertainment##
Generative Al contributes to media and entertainment in the following ways:

. Content generation: Generative Al can be used to create scripts, dialogues, or even
complete stories for films, TV shows, and games.
. Virtual reality: Generative Al can create immersive and interactive virtual environments for
games or simulations.
. News generation: Al can generate news articles or summaries based on raw data or events.

Amazon Uses Generative Al with Alexa
One of the ways Amazon is using generative Al is with the Create With Alexa feature. Create With Alexa uses
advances in conversational and generative Al to empower young storytellers to build unique stories with a
narrative arc, colorful graphics, and fun, complementary background music. The animated stories then come
to life on the screen of Amazon Echo Show devices.

To build these stories, Alexa researchers created several different content generators using generative AI.


Story generation
The story generator includes two models that use pretrained language models. The planner
model receives the customer-selected prompts and uses them to generate a longer set of
keywords that are allocated to separate scenes. The keywords constitute the story plan. The text
generator model receives the story plan and outputs the story text. It uses human-written stories
labeled according to the themes that customers will ultimately choose from, such as
underwater and enchanted forest.

Scene generation
The scene generation text is processed through two natural language processing (NLP) modules.
This lets the module determine references to pronouns and other indicative words and rewrite
the text accordingly. For example, if the mermaid mentioned in scene one is referred to as she in
scene two, the module rewrites she as the mermaid. This makes the scene generator more
efficient at interpreting the text.

Music generation
The music generation is processed from a large library of instrumental parts. At run time, the
library can combine parts automatically to create a theme and instrumental signature for each
hero character. An Al musical-arrangement system fits all the pieces together based on their
attributes, such as chord progression, rhythm, and instrument type.

A text-to-speech model computes the time it will take to read the text. The model's outputs help
to determine the duration and character of the background music. A paralinguistic-analysis
model scores the text along multiple axes, such as calm to exciting and sad to happy.
